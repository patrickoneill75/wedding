Open day-of view ↗

The day, step by step. Link the vendors, guests, groups and tables each step needs. The coordinator follows it on the day-of view and marks steps started and done; you see it here as it happens.

Guest groups

Named sets of guests to link to steps, like "Family photos: Smith side" or "Readers". Guest cohorts (the wedding party, families, each side, ceremony, reception and rehearsal dinner guests, out-of-town and international guests) and each table are ready-made and don't need a group.
